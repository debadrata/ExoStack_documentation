HIL optimizer

Proposes candidates, collects their costs and keeps the best; there is one optimizer per profile in HIL mode.

The node asks its plug-in for a candidate (get_next_candidate()), publishes it with a new k, and passes each matching cost back (submit_result()). Costs with another k are discarded. When the optimizer stops, every round and the best candidate (get_best_so_far()) are written to ~/exo_stack_resources/hil_results/HIL_<optimizer>_<cost>_…_<time>.csv.
