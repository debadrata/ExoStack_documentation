ExoStack / ROS 2 interfaces

Gait-event detector

One node per gait event: reads named channels and publishes a pulse when its plug-in fires.

Each detector passes the configured channels to its plug-in's detect() and publishes 1 when the event is confirmed by its confidence window (Fig. S1a in Configuration).

Node name<event>_event
Executablegait_event_detector via gait_event_detector_launch.py
Instances×E, one per entry of gait_events.yaml
