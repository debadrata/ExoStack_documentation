Cost evaluator

Scores each candidate over the configured gait cycles and returns the mean cost with its k.

The cost evaluator follows the planner's profile_status: for each new k it skips gait_cycles_to_skip cycles while the wearer adapts, evaluates its cost plug-in per cycle on the requested channels, external signals and gait marker, and publishes the mean over gait_cycles_to_consider cycles.

Node name<profile>_cost
Executablecost via cost_launch.py
Instances×C, one per profile with a cost
